Sell-Out Reporting Pack: Available

A Power BI report on sell-out volume and revenue: what wholesale partners and Meridian's own boutiques sold in 2025. Wholesale Sell-Out and DTC / Boutique are built as two report pages from two different fact tables: partner-reported point-of-sale data on one side, Meridian's own boutique register data on the other.

Both pages are built from fact_sell_out and fact_boutique_sales in meridian.db, loaded via Power Query and joined to their dimension tables. Revenue and units only; no cost or margin data is modeled.

2025 sell-out totaled CHF 565.6m through Wholesale and CHF 227.4m through DTC, up 7.5% and 20.2% on 2024. 119 of Meridian's 224 wholesale accounts (53%) report sell-out data; coverage runs 52-60% across all four regions and both wholesale channels.

The top 5 wholesale accounts hold 5.8% of sell-out revenue, the top 20 hold 21.2%. Both channels follow the same seasonal pattern: low from January through August, then a build through Q4 that roughly doubles revenue by December.

How it was built

Wholesale Sell-Out

Point-of-sale data reported by wholesale partners, read from fact_sell_out. Only accounts flagged reports_sellout = TRUE feed this page: 119 of 224 wholesale accounts. Cut by region, channel, collection, and partner.

DTC / Boutique

Transaction data from Meridian's own boutique register systems, read from fact_boutique_sales, Retail and Online. Covers all 56 boutiques with no partner-reporting dependency. Cut by region, channel, collection, and store.

Both fact tables are queried from meridian.db and loaded into Power BI through Power Query: fact_sell_out (34,689 rows: date, customer, SKU, units, sell-out revenue) and fact_boutique_sales (34,433 rows: date, store, SKU, units, net revenue). Each is joined in Power Query to dim_date, dim_line, and its own party table, dim_wholesale_customer for Wholesale, dim_store for DTC.

Power Query transformations: local-currency columns are dropped in favor of the CHF revenue columns already present in both fact tables; customer and store names are shortened for the report's tables and slicers (Wholesale Customer 174 → Account 174; Meridiana Boutique United Kingdom 5 → United Kingdom 5); date columns are typed and a month-name column is added for the Select Month slicer.

In the model view, both fact tables connect to dim_date on date_id, to dim_line on sku_id for collection, and to their party table on customer_id or store_id. dim_wholesale_customer and dim_store both connect onward to dim_geography (region and country) and dim_channel (Retail or Online), so region and channel filters behave the same way on both pages despite reading from two different fact tables.

The report

Both pages use the same layout: a KPI row, three charts, a monthly trend, and a ranked table. The KPI cards differ between the two pages; the charts, trend, and table read the same way on both.

KPI row: five cards plus a month selector. Wholesale Sell-Out shows Revenue, Units, Top 5 Partners Weight, Sell-Out Coverage, and Report Coverage. DTC / Boutique shows Revenue, Units, Average Price, Stores Count, and Stores Activity. The Select Month control filters the page to a single month or back to the full year.

Charts: Revenue by Region, four bars (EMEA, APAC, NA, LATAM), current year against prior year. Revenue by Collection, the same current-year/prior-year comparison as ten horizontal bars, one per collection. Retail vs Online, the same comparison across the two channel modes.

Below the charts: a twelve-month revenue trend, current year against prior year, and a ranked table next to it, Top 5 Partners on the Wholesale page, Top 5 Stores on the DTC page, each row showing revenue and growth vs last year.

Selecting a month, a region, a channel, or a partner/store row filters the whole page to that selection. Selecting a collection cross-highlights instead: every chart keeps its full total and shows the selected collection's share within it.

Controller's commentary

2025 sell-out totaled CHF 565.6m through Wholesale and CHF 227.4m through DTC, up 7.5% and 20.2% on 2024. Neither figure includes cost or margin; both are revenue and units only.

119 of Meridian's 224 wholesale accounts (53%) report sell-out data. Coverage by region: EMEA 52.8%, APAC 52.2%, NA 52.8%, LATAM 60.0% (15 accounts). By channel: Retail 55.5%, Online 47.8%.

Report Coverage measures 100%: every reporting account has at least one row of 2025 sell-out data on file. Tested against a full 12-month record instead, 97 of the 119 reporting accounts have one, 20 are missing one month, and 2 are missing two. The 22 incomplete accounts account for under CHF 100m of the CHF 565.6m total.

The top 5 wholesale accounts hold 5.8% of sell-out revenue; the top 20 hold 21.2%. Among the 119 reporting accounts, revenue ranges from CHF 3.0m to CHF 7.1m.

DTC covers 56 boutiques, each with a full year of transaction history in 2025. Boutique sales are captured directly in Meridian's own register systems rather than reported by a third party, so DTC has no equivalent of the Wholesale reporting-coverage gap.

Both channels follow the same seasonal pattern: low from January through August, then a build through Q4 that peaks in December. Wholesale's December revenue is roughly double its August low. DTC's December revenue is roughly double its February low.

LATAM shows the largest regional divergence between channels (the output of the Largest Regional Swing measure): Wholesale grew 0.1% in 2025 while DTC grew 129.8%, driven by new boutique openings including Chile 1 and Argentina 1. The FY2026 Budget Model plans a similar shift for Colombia and Thailand: reduced wholesale volume as new boutiques open in both markets. Within DTC, Online grew faster than Retail (45.3% vs 16.5%).
