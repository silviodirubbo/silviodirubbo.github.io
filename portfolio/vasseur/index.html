Wholesale Database: Available

This project demonstrates business analysis for a Wholesale Analyst role in luxury: reading sell-in and sell-out, tracking customer performance, and structuring data so a business review can actually be built on it. It's built here for Maison Vasseur, a fictitious footwear-led luxury maison with a small leather goods line, selling through 12 wholesale and franchise accounts across six EMEA regions.

This first stage is the raw source database only: three relational tables built in Excel, generated with realistic seasonality, lumpy wholesale ordering, and a month-by-month stock simulation, then packaged as a single workbook ready for Power Query. No pivots, formulas, or dashboards live in it on purpose — the sell-through, purchasing-trend, and customer-scorecard analysis is the next layer, to be built by hand on top of this data.

Data structure

Four flat tables, built to join cleanly in Power Query: two dimension-style tables (Accounts, Products) and two fact tables at different grains (monthly for the full sell-in/sell-out/stock picture, weekly for sell-out only).

Accounts12 rows

Account ID · Account Name · Region · Country · Account Type

Two wholesale/franchise accounts per region across UK & Ireland, Nordics & Baltics, DACH, France & Benelux, Iberia, and Southern Europe.

Products16 rows

Product ID · Product Name · Category · Season · Wholesale Price (CHF) · Retail Price (CHF)

Women's Footwear, Men's Footwear, and Leather Goods, weighted so footwear carries the large majority of both SKU count and revenue.

Transactions_Monthly2,478 rows

Account ID · Product ID · Month (YYYY-MM) · Sell-In Units · Sell-In Value (CHF) · Sell-Out Units · Sell-Out Value (CHF) · Stock Units · Returns Units

One row per active account-product-month. Stock rolls forward consistently: prior stock + sell-in − sell-out − returns.

Transactions_Weekly11,627 rows

Account ID · Product ID · Week (ISO YYYY-Www) · Sell-Out Units · Sell-Out Value (CHF)

Weekly sell-out only, reconciling exactly to the monthly totals — the grain a weekly business review actually runs on.

Wholesale Business Review Dashboard

A single Excel workbook built to demonstrate the core of the Wholesale Analyst role end to end, on two full years of Maison Vasseur's book: reading sell-in and sell-out, tracking sell-through, watching purchasing trends, and reporting on customer performance — the working toolkit for running a luxury wholesale and franchise business, not a generic BI showcase. It's a formatted one-page summary sitting on top of a set of native Excel pivot tables, so the same core KPI set can be sliced live, in Excel, from a full EMEA topline down to a single account or a single SKU.

Wholesale Analyst responsibility → what this file demonstrates

Business analysis across a wholesale & franchise portfolio Topline KPI view (Total / Wholesale / Franchise) plus Region and Country breakdowns — the full EMEA book at a glance.
Sell-through analysis True cumulative Sell-Through %, correctly accounting for opening stock rather than a naive sell-out ÷ sell-in ratio, plus the month-by-month cumulative trend.
Purchasing / sell-in trend monitoring Sell-In units and value vs. last year, broken out by channel, category, product, and month.
Customer performance reporting Named-account and Account Type breakdowns — Wholesale vs. Franchise, and every one of the 12 accounts individually.
Ongoing KPI monitoring & business review cadence The full KPI set — Sell-In, Sell-Out, Sell-Through %, Return % — with 2024 vs. 2025 built in throughout, ready to slice by month for a recurring review.
