Database & Business Review: Available

This project demonstrates business analysis for a Wholesale Analyst role in luxury: reading sell-in and sell-out, tracking customer performance, and structuring data so a business review can actually be built on it. It's built here for Maison Vasseur, a fictitious footwear-led luxury maison with a small leather goods line, selling through 12 wholesale and franchise accounts across six EMEA regions.

The deliverable has two parts, both below: a clean relational source database — four tables covering accounts, products, and two years of sell-in, sell-out, and stock movement — and a Wholesale Business Review Dashboard built on top of it in Power Query and native Excel PivotTables, covering sell-through, purchasing trend, and customer performance.

Wholesale Business Review Dashboard

A business review built in Power Query and native Excel PivotTables on top of the source database — Sell-In, Sell-Out, Sell-Through %, and Return %, sliced live across Account Type, Region, Category, Product, Month, and named Account, with 2024 vs. 2025 built in throughout.

Wholesale Analyst responsibility → what this file demonstrates

Business analysis, wholesale & franchise Topline (Total / Wholesale / Franchise) plus Region and Country views.
Sell-through analysis True cumulative Sell-Through %, opening-stock aware, plus the month-by-month trend.
Purchasing / sell-in trend monitoring Sell-In units & value vs. last year, by channel, category, product, and month.
Customer performance reporting Wholesale vs. Franchise, and all 12 named accounts individually.
KPI monitoring & review cadence Sell-In, Sell-Out, Sell-Through %, Return % — 2024 vs. 2025, sliceable by month.

This is a static Excel workbook — a formatted summary sheet (ONEPAGE) driven by lookups against native pivot tables (PIVOT) with live slicers — not a click-through web app. Full interactivity requires opening it in Excel.
