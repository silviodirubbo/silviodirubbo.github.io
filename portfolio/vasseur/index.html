Database & Business Review: Available

This project demonstrates business analysis for a Wholesale Analyst role in luxury: reading sell-in and sell-out, tracking customer performance, and structuring data so a business review can actually be built on it. It's built here for Maison Vasseur, a fictitious footwear-led luxury maison with a small leather goods line, selling through 12 wholesale and franchise accounts across six EMEA regions.

Two parts, both below: a clean relational source database, and a Wholesale Business Review Dashboard built on top of it in Power Query and native Excel PivotTables.

Wholesale Business Review Dashboard

A dashboard covering the core of the role: Sell-In, Sell-Out, Sell-Through %, and Returns — sliceable by month, season, division, region, channel, account, and product.

What this dashboard is built for

  • Analyze wholesale and franchise customer performance, sell-through, and purchasing trends.
  • Prepare and maintain regular reporting tools and dashboards for Wholesale, Merchandising, and senior management teams.
  • Gather and consolidate customer data to produce weekly and monthly business reviews.
  • Support preparation for commercial meetings with Women's, Men's, and Leather Goods teams by providing insights and performance analysis.
  • Monitor key business KPIs and proactively highlight areas requiring attention or action.

Data structure

Four flat, relational tables — no formulas baked into the file itself. The real data work — merges, transformations, and the native PivotTables behind the Business Review above — happens in Power Query on top of this source, not inside these sheets.
